Privacy

Your writing stays yours.

No account, no profile, no sync you did not ask for. As Told is a private writing space that works the moment you open it — and keeps what you write on the device you wrote it on.

No account required.

There is no sign-up screen, no email to verify, no profile in somebody's cloud. Open the app and start writing. Nothing about your notes is tied to an identity, because there is no identity to tie them to.

Your note library lives on your device.

Notes are stored locally on your iPhone. They are not uploaded for us to read, index, mine for advertising, or use to train anything. Search and the calendar run against your own device, which is also why they stay fast and work without a connection.

Face ID when you want it.

Some writing is more private than the rest. Turn on Face ID and As Told asks for it when you reopen the app, so a note left mid-sentence is not the first thing a borrowed phone shows.

The honest part about voice.

Voice is the one place where something leaves the phone. Transcription needs more computing power than a phone should spend on it, so the audio goes over an encrypted connection, gets turned into text, and is not kept. Your notes are never sent along with it as context.

Plenty of apps say "nothing ever leaves your device" and quietly mean "except this." We would rather tell you which part is which. Read the full privacy detail →
