As Told · Privacy

Private by design

Your writing isn't an account.

As Told is built so your notes stay yours. No sign-up, no cloud library of your writing, and voice that's transcribed faithfully and not kept. This page explains exactly what that means.

Last updated · August 2026

The short version

  • No account. There's no sign-up, email, or password to write something down.
  • Your notes stay on your device. They're stored locally on your iPhone — there is no cloud database of your notes.
  • Voice is transcribed, not kept. Audio is used only to produce the transcript, then deleted.
  • Nothing is logged. The transcription service never stores your audio, transcript, or note text.
  • Optional Face ID. Lock the app, and your notes are hidden in the app switcher until you unlock.

Where your notes live

Your note library is kept locally, in the app's own storage on your iPhone. As Told doesn't require an account and doesn't sync your notes to a server you don't control. Because there's no account, there's nothing tying your notes to an identity.

If a future version adds optional device-to-device sync, it will be exactly that — optional, clearly explained, and off unless you turn it on.

Voice & transcription

When you record a voice note, the audio is sent securely to a transcription service for one purpose: turning your speech into text. As soon as the transcript is produced, the recording is deleted. The transcription service is set up so it does not store or log your audio, your transcript, your note title, or your note text — only anonymous technical information needed to run reliably.

Your speech is transcribed in your own words. Natural punctuation, capitalization, and paragraph breaks are added so a spoken thought reads like written language — but As Told does not translate, summarize, rewrite, polish, or grammar-correct what you said. The transcript is meant to be what you actually spoke — in English, Telugu, Hindi, or a natural mix — inserted directly into your note as ordinary, editable text.

To transcribe well, the service receives your recorded audio, fixed transcription instructions, and allowed-language hints. It is not sent the rest of your note as context.

Face ID & the app switcher

You can optionally require Face ID (or your device passcode) to open As Told. When the lock is on and the app leaves the screen, your note content is covered before iOS takes its app-switcher snapshot — so your writing isn't left visible behind the lock. Notes are revealed only after you authenticate.

Analytics

As Told favors Apple's own privacy-respecting diagnostics. If any product analytics are ever added, they will never include your note text, transcript text, audio, or search queries.

Children

As Told is a general-audience notes app and isn't directed at children, and it doesn't collect personal information to build a profile of anyone.

Changes to this page

If these practices change, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will be reflected before a new version relies on them.
