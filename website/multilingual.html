Multilingual

Telugu stays Telugu. Hindi stays Hindi.

Most dictation assumes one language per sentence, picks the one it expects, and converts the rest into English without telling you. As Told is built for how people actually talk — English, Telugu, Hindi, and the switching between them mid-sentence — and it hands the words back in the script you said them in.

Verbatim

Fidelity is the whole product.

Your speech comes back in the script you spoke it in — Latin, Telugu, or Devanagari — punctuated so it reads, and otherwise untouched. Not tidied. Not shortened. Not quietly moved into English because English was easier to handle.

A translation is a different sentence wearing your meaning. As Told would rather give you the sentence.
