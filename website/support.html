Help

A quiet app. Help when you need it.

As Told is meant to stay out of your way, so there isn't much to learn. Here are the questions people ask most.

How do I add a note by voice?

Open a note, place the cursor where you want the words to go, and tap the microphone. Speak naturally, then tap Done. As Told transcribes what you said and inserts it right into the note as ordinary, editable text.

Which languages can I speak?

English, Telugu, and Hindi — plus the natural mix between them, like Telugu + English or Hindi + English. You can switch languages mid-sentence and As Told keeps it as you said it. See how multilingual capture works →

Does it translate or rewrite what I say?

No. Voice capture is designed to preserve your words, not to translate, summarize, polish, or grammar-correct them. What you spoke is what lands in your note.

Where are my notes stored? Is there a backup or sync?

Your notes are stored locally on your iPhone — there's no account and no cloud database of your notes. In this version there's no built-in sync or backup service, so your notes live on your device. Standard device backups you already use are the way to keep a copy.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start writing. There's no sign-up, email, or password.

Why does voice need an internet connection?

Typing, editing, browsing, search, and the calendar all work offline. Only the voice transcription step needs a connection, because the audio is sent securely to be turned into text. If you're offline when you finish a recording, As Told won't lose it silently — you'll get a clear option to retry or discard.

How do I turn on Face ID lock?

Go to Profile → Settings and turn on Lock with Face ID. When it's on, your notes are hidden in the app switcher and revealed only after you authenticate. Your device passcode works as a fallback.

How do I switch between Light and Dark?

Go to Profile → Settings → Theme and choose Light, Dark, or Use device settings. “Use device settings” follows your iPhone automatically.

I deleted a note by accident. Can I get it back?

Right after you swipe to delete, an Undo appears for a short time — tap it to restore the note exactly as it was. Once that window passes, the note is removed.

Still need a hand?

Email support@astold.app and we'll get back to you.

Terms

As Told is provided as-is for personal note-taking. You own the notes you create. Because your notes are stored on your device and aren't synced to our servers, keeping your own device backups is the way to protect against data loss. Full terms will be published here before release; for anything urgent in the meantime, contact support above.
